SpotOn does not inspect, verify or guarantee any listed space beyond what the host tells us. Check the listing details and photos before you book.

06Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel a confirmed booking yourself from the app, for a full refund including the service fee, up until 24 hours before it's scheduled to start. We reserve the right to change that window going forward; the number in effect at the time you book is the one that applies to that booking.

Once you're inside that window, or after a booking has started, self-serve cancellation isn't available. Email team.spotonapp@gmail.com and we'll look at it case by case.

One case is automatic on our end: if a host never finishes Stripe onboarding in time to receive a booking's payout, we automatically refund the renter in full and cancel the booking.

If the space isn't available when you arrive

If you arrive and the space is unavailable, blocked, or the host refuses you access, email team.spotonapp@gmail.com within 24 hours of the booking's start time, ideally with a photo. We'll review it, and if we confirm the problem, we'll refund you in full and withhold the host's payout for that booking.

If you don't show up

A booking reserves the space for you for the whole window you paid for, so the host can't rent it to anyone else. If you don't show up, or don't use the full window, you won't be refunded once you're inside the 24-hour cancellation window.

If a host cancels

Hosts shouldn't cancel confirmed bookings. If a host has to, the renter gets a full refund. Repeated host cancellations can lead to the host's listings being removed or their account being suspended.
